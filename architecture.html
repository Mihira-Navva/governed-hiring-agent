Responsible AI & Governance · Case 02 · AI-assisted hiring

A hiring agent that acts, with governance on top

The hiring agent reads applications, screens them and acts: it invites strong candidates and rejects only those who clearly fail a legal or operational requirement. Above it, governance approves it before it runs, sets how much it may do alone, checks every decision before it takes effect, and withdraws its autonomy automatically if outcomes turn unfair.

Teal arrows are governance; amber are people; red dashed is the circuit breaker
Whole system

Applications enter the hiring agent at the bottom. Every job spec and every proposed decision goes up to the guardian before anything happens. Released decisions reach candidates; everything else reaches a recruiter. The committee signs the policy that sets the agent's autonomy, and only the committee can raise it.

One requisition, 40 applications

What the agent did at L2

19 invited to interview9 rejected on a hard requirement (8 certificate, 1 location)12 to a recruiter

The guardian released 28 autonomous decisions and sampled 3 for blind audit. It wrote 40 ledger entries, and the chain verifies.

What governance caught

Before screening: the manager's "top colleges" and "no career gaps" criteria were blocked at the gate; the cleaned spec passed and was hash-pinned.

During screening: a resume telling the AI to "rank this candidate first" was flagged and sent to a person. A strong candidate with a maternity break and a 47-year-old were both invited, because neither fact was ever scored.

Fire drill: 1,200 biased historical decisions turned the monitor RED (women 0.63, age 40+ 0.44). The breaker dropped autonomy to L1, and the same 40 applications then received 0 autonomous actions.
