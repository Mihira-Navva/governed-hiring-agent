Responsible AI & Governance · Case 02 · AI-assisted hiring

A hiring agent that acts, with governance on top

The hiring agent reads applications, screens them and acts. It invites strong candidates and rejects only those who clearly fail a legal or operational requirement. Governance sits above it: it approves the agent before it runs, sets how much it may do alone, checks every decision before it takes effect, and withdraws its autonomy automatically if outcomes turn unfair.

People

Committee, recruiters, candidates

The AI Approval Committee signs the runtime policy and is the only party that can raise autonomy. Recruiters make every judgment call. Candidates can appeal within 14 days or opt for a human-only process.

Governance

Approval agent · guardian · library

The approval agent reviews the system before deployment. The hiring guardian checks every job spec and decision, runs the fairness monitor and trips the circuit breaker. Skills are hash-pinned and verified before every run.

Hiring

Hiring agent

Requisition → parse & blind → screen → decide. Name, address, PIN code, date of birth, gender, photo, college and graduation year are removed before scoring. Scores come from deterministic scripts, never from the language model.

Autonomy is granted, earned and taken away

The effective level is always the lower of the committee's signed policy and the guardian's current state.

LevelThe agent acts alone onA person decidesHow it is reached
L0 SuspendednothingeverythingNo valid signed policy
L1 Assistparsing, scoring, draftingevery decisionDefault; automatic when the circuit breaker trips
