How it works

Three layers of protection, built in.

The AI does the repetitive work. The guardian watches the AI. People stay in charge.

01 · Before it starts

Approved before it runs

An approval agent reviews the AI against company policy and the law. A human committee signs off and decides how much the AI may do on its own.

02 · Every decision

Every decision checked

Names, age, gender, address and college are hidden before scoring. The guardian checks each decision before it reaches a candidate, and blocks unfair job criteria like "top colleges only".

03 · Always on

Auto-stop if it turns unfair

The guardian keeps measuring outcomes for women, older applicants, regions and disability. If a group is treated unfairly, it switches the AI to assist-only mode instantly.

Human in charge The AI acts alone on only two things: inviting strong candidates, and rejecting people missing a legally required qualification (with a reason and a 14-day appeal). Everything else goes to a recruiter.
